CDC replication · IBM i to Snowflake · Apache-2.0

Replicate IBM i journals to Snowflake, with separate evidence for reading and delivery.

For data teams and IBM i operators who need a Snowflake mirror of journaled Db2 for i tables on their own AWS or GCP infrastructure. Quadringent uses change data capture on IBM i (formerly AS/400 and iSeries) journals: an open-source engine, API and cockpit observe journal reading, raw storage, durable checkpoints and loading separately. Delivery is checked by reading back the source, history and mirror.

How it works

Four stages, observed separately.

Active capture does not mean Snowflake is up to date. Quadringent observes each stage independently and never infers one from another.

  1. 01

    IBM i journal

    A Java/JTOpen reader reads journal entries over TLS, starting from the last acquired position.

    Producesjournal entries
  2. 02

    Raw storage

    Each batch is retained unchanged in S3 or GCS, with a receipt, before loading.

    ProducesRAW files + receipts
  3. 03

    Checkpoint

    The read position is recorded outside the reader. Pausing or restarting resumes from that point.

    Producesdurable position
  4. 04

    Snowflake mirror

    The loader applies batches to history and mirror tables; readback compares the source, history and mirror.

    Produceshistory + mirror

Cockpit

Distinguish activity from evidence.

The desktop cockpit presents each connection and table with its state in words, then available actions and their observed effects.

  • Connections and tables

    State, lag, throughput, source and Snowflake row counts, last arrival. One banner highlights what needs verification.

  • Checkpoints and recovery

    The read position is retained outside the reader. Pause and resume are controlled from the cockpit; retention of RAW data and checkpoints after complete site loss was verified on historical versions only (see Qualification). The Logs tab lists timestamped table events, as in the adjacent demonstration.

  • Confirmations

    Removing a table, restarting an initial copy, replaying a journal range or suspending the entire organization requires human approval. An agent connected through MCP cannot approve its own action without explicit administrator preauthorization. Every call is audited.

  • Missing data stays visible

    The Evidence tab separates journal capture, Snowflake delivery and reconciliation, with timestamps and measured scope. A missing value displays “—”; an unmeasured stage stays “Not measured”.

Install Community

Try the demo, then install on your cloud.

A real site requires an IBM i journal and TLS access, a scoped Snowflake role, raw storage and limited cloud permissions. Check compatibility and prerequisites first, and read the security policy before exposing the cockpit.

Qualification

What was measured, where, and when.

Each result stays bound to its version, targets and observation window: DEV/INT tests on isolated installations using synthetic tables. The harness threshold is 10 s per write, and each result names where its clock starts. These operator-observed results do not qualify production.

0.3.4 · published DEV/INT preview · observed 2026-10-12

  • ObservedTyped CDC readback. INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE on four targets: 136 history images and 110 mirror rows per site, all seven business columns matched, 0 duplicate event IDs.

  • ObservedApplication reinstallation. CLI uninstall then install kept PostgreSQL volumes, keys and pipeline identities. Pipelines stayed paused; renewed CDC was not verified.
  • Not establishedReader outage. Not established on 0.3.4; observed on 0.3.2.
  • Not establishedFresh installation and initial copy. Not established on 0.3.4; observed on 0.3.2 with 2 tables and 110 rows per site.
  • Not establishedComplete site loss and reconstruction. Not established on 0.3.4.

Open limitations

What remains to be demonstrated.

These known items are tracked and inform operational decisions.

  • To repeatReader failure on 0.3.4. Real reader outages passed on 0.3.2; the current release still needs its own outage and source-write readback.
  • To repeatFresh installation and initial copy on 0.3.4. Observed on 0.3.2; the current release still needs its own fresh installation and initial copy.
  • To demonstrateComplete site reconstruction. Repeat loss, reconstruction and renewed CDC with the exact current release. Historical rebuilds apply only to their original versions.
  • Above thresholdCold-warehouse latency. The first 0.3.4 trial exceeded 10 s on three targets from SQL start. All four exceeded 10 s when source-client startup was included.
  • Not qualifiedProduction. No PROD qualification; the chart rejects promotion to PROD.
  • To protectCockpit access. Unauthenticated loopback by default; secure access before exposing it.
  • BoundedLatency. No universal ten-second guarantee for every workload or startup.
  • PartialEvidence per table. Separate stages are available; capture and reconciliation still need their own observations. Missing measurements remain explicit.
  • PartialHistorical archives. Historical RAW archives remain incomplete on two test sites.
  • DesktopWorkstations. Cockpit designed for desktop workstations, with a 1440 px keyboard-and-mouse reference; other sizes are not qualified.

Model

Start freely, get help when needed.

The software remains open source. Proposed assistance would cover installation and validation work. No paid offer has been launched or priced.

    Community

    Engine, API, cockpit and chart under Apache-2.0. IBM i, cloud and Snowflake costs remain the operator’s responsibility.

  2. First proposed service

    Installation and pilot

    On your infrastructure, with table scope, acceptance criteria and evidence deliverables agreed before engagement.

  3. Next

    Support

    A separate agreement after the pilot, based on observed needs.

  4. If demand is confirmed

    Managed hosting

    Considered only after a real need is confirmed.
